## Reflection

During this requirements exercise, our team identified the key stakeholders involved in the development and use of the game, including players, developers, designers, testers, and project managers. Considering these stakeholders helped us better understand who interacts with the system and what expectations they might have from the game. This allowed us to think not only about gameplay mechanics but also about usability, testing, and project coordination.

By defining epics and user stories, we were able to break down the overall idea of the game into smaller, manageable features such as controlling the spaceship, shooting asteroids, and tracking the player's score. Writing acceptance criteria using the Given–When–Then format helped clarify how each feature should behave from the player's perspective and ensured that the requirements were clear and testable.

This process also improved collaboration within the team. Discussing and refining user stories together allowed us to share ideas and align our understanding of the system requirements. As a result, the requirements became more structured and easier to translate into implementation tasks. Overall, this exercise highlighted the importance of clear and well-defined requirements in guiding the development process and reducing misunderstandings during implementation.
